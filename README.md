# ttybitnik/lab.studies

A collection of laboratory studies bridging computational knowledge and experimental practices. Each study project is a hands-on investigation of a core concept or foundational text.

> The computer revolution is a revolution in the way we think and in the
> way we express what we think. The essence of this change is the
> emergence of what might best be called procedural epistemology—the
> study of the structure of knowledge from an imperative point of view,
> as opposed to the more declarative point of view taken by classical
> mathematical subjects. Mathematics provides a framework for dealing
> precisely with notions of 'what is.' Computation provides a framework
> for dealing precisely with notions of 'how to.'

> — Gerald Jay Sussman & Harold Abelson in [SICP](https://mitpress.mit.edu/sicp).
